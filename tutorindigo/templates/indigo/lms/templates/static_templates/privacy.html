This policy explains what personal information Stem Quest Academy, a Scient Systems company based in Ohio, USA, collects on this learning site, why we collect it, and what choices you have. We collect what we need to run your courses, and we do not sell personal information.

1. What we collect

  • Account details. Your name, username, email address and password. Your password is stored in a scrambled form that we cannot read. You may also choose to add details to your profile, such as your country.
  • Learning activity. The courses you enroll in, your progress, your answers, your grades, your certificates, and anything you post in a course.
  • Membership and payments. Which plan you have, when it renews or ends, and a record of your payments. Card details go straight to our payment provider, Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number.
  • AI tool use. When you use an AI tool in a course, the text you type is sent to the AI service that produces the answer. We also keep a count of how much you use these tools.
  • Technical information. Your IP address, your browser and device type, and logs of the pages you request. We use these to keep the site secure and to fix problems.
  • Messages you send us. For example, when you ask for help.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account and let you sign in.
  • To deliver courses, save your progress, and issue certificates.
  • To manage memberships and take payments.
  • To send emails the service needs, such as account activation, password resets and payment receipts.
  • To keep the site safe and to stop cheating and misuse.
  • To understand how courses are used so we can improve them.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it to show advertising from other companies.

3. Who we share it with

We share personal information only where the service needs it:

  • Stripe, to process payments.
  • AI services such as Google Gemini and OpenRouter, which receive the text typed into an AI tool so they can answer. We do not send them your name or email address with it.
  • Hosting and email providers that store the site's data and deliver our emails.
  • Your school or organization, if it gave you access. It can see your enrollment and progress in the courses it gave you.
  • Course staff, who can see your work and progress in the courses they teach.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or when it is needed to protect someone's safety.

The companies that work for us may use the information only to do that work.

4. Children's privacy

Many of our students are under 18, so we keep the information we ask for to a minimum.

  • A student under 18 needs the permission of a parent or guardian to use the site.
  • A child under 13 may have an account only if a parent or guardian creates and manages it, or if the child's school provides it.
  • A parent or guardian can ask to see their child's information, ask us to correct or delete it, or tell us to stop collecting it. Use the contact details below.
  • If we learn that a child under 13 has an account without a parent's or a school's consent, we will delete it.

5. Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in, to remember choices such as your language and light or dark mode, and to protect the site. The site does not work without them. We do not use advertising cookies.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account and learning records for as long as your account is open, so your progress and certificates stay available. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information, apart from records the law requires us to keep, such as payment records.

7. How we protect it

The site uses encrypted connections. Access to personal information is limited to the people who need it to run the service. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you as the law requires.

8. Your choices

  • You can see and change your account details in your account settings.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct or delete it.
  • You can cancel a paid membership at any time from your membership page.

Depending on where you live, the law may give you more rights over your information. We will respect them.

9. Where your information is kept

Our servers are in the United States. If you use the site from another country, your information is transferred to and stored in the United States.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page, and if the change is important we will tell you by email or on the site.

11. Contact us

Questions or requests about your information? Use the contact form on our website. See also our Terms of Service.
