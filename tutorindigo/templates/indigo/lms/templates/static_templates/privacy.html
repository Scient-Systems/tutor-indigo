How we collect, use, and protect information at SQA LMS. Effective October 26, 2026.

The short version

  • SQA LMS is a learning platform for students, many of them kids. We collect only what we need to run courses and memberships and to track learning progress.
  • We never show ads, never sell data, and add no analytics or advertising trackers of our own.
  • A student under 13 needs a parent or guardian, or their school, to create and manage their account.
  • You can see, correct, or delete your information any time — parents can do the same for their child — by emailing ask@scientsystems.com.

Who we are

SQA LMS (this website) is operated by Scient Systems ("we," "us") as part of its STEM Quest Academy education programs. We are based in Ohio, in the United States, and welcome students from other countries. For anything in this policy, contact us at ask@scientsystems.com.

What we collect

  • Account information. A name, username, email address, and a password we store only in hashed form — never as plain text.
  • Profile details. Anything you choose to add to your profile, such as your country.
  • Learning progress. The courses you enroll in, your progress, answers, grades, and certificates.
  • Discussion posts. What you post in course discussions. Other learners and the staff in that course can see these posts.
  • Membership and payments. Your plan, when it renews or ends, and a record of your payments. Card details go straight to Stripe, our payment provider. We never see or store your full card number.
  • AI tool use. When you use an AI tool in a course, the text you type is sent to the AI service that produces the answer. We also keep a count of how much you use these tools.
  • Messages you send us. For example, when you ask for help.
  • Technical logs. Your IP address, browser and device type, and logs of the pages you visit and your activity in courses. We use these to keep the site secure, fix problems, and understand how courses are used so we can improve them.

What we never do

  • No advertising of any kind on the platform.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of personal information for marketing.
  • No analytics or advertising trackers of our own. (Course videos play through an outside video service, which may set its own cookies.)
  • No names or email addresses sent to AI services: they receive only the text typed into a course's AI tool.

Children's privacy

Many of our students are under 18, and we designed SQA LMS with that in mind. We collect only the information described above — the minimum we need to teach courses and show progress.

  • A student under 18 needs the permission of a parent or guardian to use the platform.
  • A child under 13 may have an account only if a parent or guardian creates and manages it, or if the child's school provides it.
  • If we learn that a child under 13 has an account without a parent's or school's consent, we will delete it.

A parent or guardian can review their child's information, ask us to correct it, ask us to stop collecting it, or ask us to delete the account entirely by emailing ask@scientsystems.com. We honor these requests for all families, wherever they live.

How our emails work

We send the emails the service needs — such as account activation, password resets, payment receipts, and course notifications — to the email address on your account. They are delivered through our email provider.

Service providers

We use a small number of companies to run the platform. Each receives only what it needs to do its job:

  • Stripe — processes membership payments.
  • Google Gemini and OpenRouter — receive the text typed into a course's AI tools so they can answer. We don't send your name or email address with it.
  • A video service — plays course videos, and may set its own cookies.
  • Our hosting and content delivery providers — run the servers in the United States that store the platform's data, and deliver the site's files.
  • Our email provider — delivers the platform's emails.

The companies that work for us may use the information only to do that work.

Who else can see your information

  • Course staff can see your work and progress in the courses they teach.
  • Your school or organization, if it gave you access, can see your enrollment and progress in the courses it provided.
  • Other learners in a course can see your username and what you post in its discussions.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or when it is needed to protect someone's safety.

Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in, to remember choices such as your language and light or dark mode, and to protect the site. The site does not work without them. We set no advertising or analytics cookies. Course videos play through an outside video service, which may set its own cookies.

How we protect information

  • Passwords are stored hashed, never as plain text.
  • All traffic to the site is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Card details are handled by Stripe and never reach our servers.
  • Access is role-based: course staff see only the courses they teach, and personal information is limited to the people who need it to run the service.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you as the law requires.

How long we keep information

  • Account and learning records: for as long as your account is open, so your progress and certificates stay available.
  • When you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information, apart from records the law requires us to keep, such as payment records.

Your choices

  • You can see and change your account details in your account settings.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct or delete it.
  • You can cancel a paid membership any time from your membership page.

International families

We operate from the United States, and information is processed and stored on servers located there. If you use SQA LMS from another country, your information will be handled in the United States as described in this policy, with the same protections for every family. Depending on where you live, the law may give you more rights over your information, and we will respect them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page with a new effective date. Meaningful changes to how we handle your information will be communicated to members by email or on the site.

Effective October 26, 2026. See also our Terms of Service.
